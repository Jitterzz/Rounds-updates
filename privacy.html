Rounds

Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

Rounds is an app for keeping track of a lawn route and who has paid. It is made by Davidrtx. This page explains what Rounds does with the information you put in it.

The short version

  • Your houses and payments are stored only on your phone.
  • Rounds has no accounts, no ads and no tracking.
  • Nothing you enter is sent to the developer, and nothing is sold.
  • Your records leave your phone only when you choose: a backup, an export, or a call, text or map you open.

What Rounds keeps on your phone

Rounds saves your records in its own private storage on your phone, where other apps can't read them:

  • Houses: customer names, addresses, phone numbers, notes (like gate codes), route day and order, price, and how often you visit.
  • Payments: amounts, how and when each house paid, notes, and check numbers and bank names when you enter or scan them.
  • Settings: your theme, reminders, fingerprint lock, and where automatic backups are saved.

Rounds doesn't read your location or contacts. It only opens files you pick yourself, like a backup to restore or a spreadsheet to import.

Your customers' information

The names, addresses and phone numbers you add belong to your customers. Rounds keeps them on your phone and uses them only to show your route and to open a call, text or directions when you tap.

When information leaves your phone

Only when you do something that sends it:

  • Backups: automatic backup and Back up now save a copy of your records to the place you pick, such as Google Drive or a folder on your phone. That copy is then kept by that service under its own privacy policy.
  • Exports: a spreadsheet you export goes wherever you save it.
  • Calls, texts and directions: these open your phone, messaging or maps app with that house's number or address. Those apps follow their own privacy policies.
  • Your phone's backup: if backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include Rounds' records in your phone's backup to your Google account.

Scanning checks

Scanning a check uses Google's document scanner and text reader from Google Play services. The picture is read on your phone, and Rounds deletes its copy as soon as the check number, bank and amount are read. The picture and the text read from it aren't sent anywhere.

To keep these tools working, Google Play services may send Google basic technical details, such as your phone's model, Android version, app version and how well the scanner performed. Google explains this on its ML Kit data disclosure page.

Fingerprint lock and reminders

The fingerprint lock uses Android's own fingerprint and PIN screen. Rounds never sees or stores your fingerprint or PIN.

Route-day reminders are set up on your phone. Nothing is sent to a server to make them work.

Purchases

If you buy Rounds Pro or anything else in Rounds, Google Play handles the payment. Rounds only gets confirmation of what you bought, and never sees your card or payment details. Google's privacy policy covers the payment itself.

Updates

Rounds from Google Play is updated through Google Play. Copies installed from GitHub instead check github.com for new versions. That check sends no records, only the usual details any web request includes, like your IP address, which GitHub handles under its privacy statement.

Deleting your information

You can delete houses and clear payments in the app. Uninstalling Rounds, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, removes everything Rounds keeps on your phone. Backups and exports you saved elsewhere stay there until you delete them. The developer never receives your records, so there is nothing to delete on the developer's side.

Children

Rounds is made for adults running a lawn service. It isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
